L Browser - Liquid Glass UI

Translucent Glass
Base glass effect with 24px blur and translucent white background. Perfect for overlays and panels.
Dark Glass
Dark variant with translucent black background. Maintains the same smooth transitions.
Pure White
Nearly opaque white glass (95% opacity) for solid overlays with glass aesthetic.
Pure Black
Nearly opaque black glass (95% opacity) for dark mode solid overlays.
Jelly Glass
Advanced glass with light bending simulation. Features gradient overlay and dynamic light sweep on hover.
Physical Properties
Mimics real glass with inset shadows for depth and light refraction effects.
Design Philosophy: The Liquid Glass UI uses only pure black (#000000), pure white (#FFFFFF), and translucent variations. All effects feature smooth, liquid-smooth animations with custom cubic-bezier curves for an organic feel. Hover over the cards to see the interactive effects!
